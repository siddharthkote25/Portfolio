Engineering Workflow

Design to Fabrication Methodology

Strict IPC-compliant design checklist guaranteeing first-pass PCB fabrication success.

01

Schematic & ERC Validation

Hierarchical sheet partitioning, symbol creation, power flag checks, and 100% clean Electrical Rules Check.

02

Mechanical & Footprint Check

Pinout 1:1 verification against manufacturer datasheets, IPC-7351 footprint sizing, and board outline constraints.

03

Placement & High-di/dt Loops

Decoupling caps positioned immediately next to IC pins; crystal oscillators isolated; power & analog domains separated.

04

Trace Routing & Return Paths

Manual routing of high-speed/critical nets, continuous ground return copper pours, thermal vias, and silkscreen cleanup.

05

Fabrication & Gerber X2 Export

DRC with zero warnings, Excellon drill generation, IPC-D-356 netlist, and assembly BOM/CPL files.
