Engineering Workflow

From Concept to Fabricated PCB

A systematic KiCad design methodology ensuring clean schematics and first-pass manufacturing yield.

01

Schematic Capture & ERC

Hierarchical sheet partitioning, component selection, net labels, power flags, and electrical rules check.

02

Footprint & Board Setup

Verification of pinouts, creating custom footprints, defining board outline and net class constraints.

03

Component Placement

Strategic placement placing decoupling caps and crystals directly next to MCU pins, separating power & signal paths.

04

Routing & Copper Fills

Manual routing of power and signal nets, ground copper planes on top/bottom layers, thermal vias, and silkscreen cleanup.

05

DRC & Gerber Export

Rigorous Design Rule Checks (0 errors), 3D clearance inspection, and generating Gerber RS-274X & Drill files.
