Engineering Workflow

From Concept to Assembled PCB

A systematic design methodology guaranteeing first-pass manufacturing yield.

01

Schematic & ERC

Component selection based on supply chain availability, symbol creation, Electrical Rules Check.

02

Stackup & Constraints

Substrate thickness, dielectric constant, copper weights, and controlled trace impedance calculation.

03

Routing & Thermal Plan

Critical nets first, ground plane continuity, differential pairing, and thermal dissipation paths.

04

DRC & DFM Verification

Fabrication clearance checks, solder mask expansion, annular ring verification, and 3D enclosure check.

05

Fab & Assembly

Automated Gerber X2, drill files, BOM, and Pick-and-Place (CPL) package generation for seamless fab.
